# Product Language & Visual Metaphors

## Product Statement
> **Wishcraft is Pi’s animated operator layer — intent, skills, ideas, guardrails, and session state made visible and controllable without turning Pi into an IDE.**

---

## The Five Pillars

1. **Deck**
   - *Definition*: The unified interactive control surface (`Alt+P` / `/wishcraft`).
   - *Metaphor*: The operator's command bridge or flight deck.
   - *Key Characteristic*: Single continuous outer frame; keyboard-first navigation with fuzzy palette (`/`).

2. **Signal**
   - *Definition*: The motion-aware animated powerline and status surface (`/signal`).
   - *Metaphor*: A living data track with connective conduits and traveling pulses.
   - *Key Characteristic*: 3 lanes (Left: Model/Git, Center: Live Activity, Right: Context/Queue) that animate only during real session events.

3. **Motion**
   - *Definition*: The centralized, event-driven terminal animation engine.
   - *Metaphor*: Kinetic resonance — purposeful energy that illustrates data movement.
   - *Key Characteristic*: Strict 0 FPS idle guarantee; semantic event routing; multi-cadence scheduling.

4. **Craft**
   - *Definition*: Integrated workflows for Skills, Ideas, Guardrails, and session tools.
   - *Metaphor*: Artisan tools, tailored playbooks, and safety guardrails.
   - *Key Characteristic*: Split-pane workbench; inline wizards; instant health checks.

5. **Appearance**
   - *Definition*: Ten signature structural presets, semantic design tokens, and the Motion Gallery.
   - *Metaphor*: Curated instruments with distinct personalities.
   - *Key Characteristic*: Complete decoupling — any preset can be combined with custom palettes, motions, or glyphs.
